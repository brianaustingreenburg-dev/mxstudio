Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

JefeTech is an app made by MX Studio, owned and run by Brian Lalo in Acapulco, Guerrero, Mexico ("MX Studio," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information JefeTech collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the JefeTech app, the La Forja dashboard, and myjefetech.app.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account and business details: your name, company name, phone number, email, trade, and city.
  • Crew information you add: names, roles, phone numbers, hours worked, and job assignments.
  • Job information: addresses, materials, change orders, photos, notes, invoices, and schedules.
  • Safety records: checklists, incident logs, and any injury or hospital details you choose to record.
  • Messages and questions you type into the AI assistant.

Information collected when you use the app:

  • Location, only when you use a feature that needs it (for example, emergency location sharing, weather, or the satellite roof viewer).
  • Device and usage information, such as device type, browser, app version, and error reports.

Payment information: Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment information you give them.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the app and its features for you and your crew.
  • To process your subscription, payments, and refunds.
  • To give you support over phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To keep the app safe, fix problems, and make it better.
  • To send you important notices about your account or changes to the app.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your crew's information for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run JefeTech:

  • Service providers that host the app, process payments (Stripe), send messages, or power the AI assistant. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Payroll and accounting tools you connect, such as Gusto, QuickBooks, or Deel, when you choose to export data to them.
  • Sales partners of MX Studio may see the business contact details needed to sign you up and support you. They do not get access to your jobs or crew data.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.
  • A new owner, if MX Studio or JefeTech is sold or merged. This policy would still apply to your information.

4. Offline data on your device

JefeTech works without signal. To do that, some information (such as jobs, checklists, and blueprints) is saved on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to see it. Use a screen lock on every device your crew uses.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. After you cancel, we delete or anonymize it within a reasonable time, unless we must keep some records longer for tax, legal, or dispute reasons.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export, or delete your information, or to stop using it for a purpose. You can also withdraw a permission, such as location, in your device settings. Contact us using the details below and we will answer within the time the law requires. If you are in Mexico, see our Aviso de Privacidad for your ARCO rights. If you are in California or another place with its own privacy law, you have the rights that law gives you, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and business measures to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

8. Children

JefeTech is a business tool for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. International use

MX Studio is based in Mexico and serves customers in Mexico and the United States. Your information may be stored or processed in either country or wherever our service providers operate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make an important change, we will post it here with a new date and let you know in the app or by message.
