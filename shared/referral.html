6The rules

  • 7% of the subscription money paid by every customer the Partner you referred signs.
  • Paid monthly, straight to your bank account, after the customer's payment clears. Refunds don't count.
  • It lasts as long as that Partner is actively selling. If they stop, the 7% stops.
  • The person you refer must be new to MX Studio and join as a Partner. You can't refer yourself.
  • MX Studio can update the program with notice. Referrals already active keep their terms.
  • 3% bonus: when a Partner you referred refers a new Partner, you also get 3% of that new Partner's customers' subscription payments, as long as that Partner is active. One level only.
