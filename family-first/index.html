Set Up Family First ✝

Follow these steps to activate every safety feature for your family. Takes about 5 minutes. Works on any phone, anywhere in the world.

✓
Install Family First on your phone
Done
▼

You're already here — which means you're one tap away from having this app on your home screen like any native app. No App Store. No download fee. Just add it.

📱 iPhone (Safari)
Tap Share → Add to Home Screen
🤖 Android (Chrome)
Tap ⋮ → Add to Home Screen
💻 Desktop
Bookmark or install as PWA from browser bar
✝ Tip: Share this link with every family member so everyone installs it. The app works the same on every device in every country.
2
Add your family members
To do
▼

Your family circle is the heart of the app. Add each person — parents, kids, grandparents, pets. Each member gets their own profile, location dot on the map, and check-in status.

Who to add: Mom, Dad, kids, grandparents, the dog. Anyone you want on your live family map. Each person installs the app on their phone and your circles sync.
3
Allow location access on every phone
Critical
▼

GPS tracking only works when each family member's phone has location access turned on. This is the most important setup step — without it, the map and SOS won't have coordinates.

📱 iPhone: Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Safari → While Using the App (or Always)
🤖 Android: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Location → Allow
Why we need this: Location data never leaves your family's private circle. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers. Full privacy. Always. ✝
4
Test the SOS emergency button
Important
▼

The SOS button is the most important feature in this app. When triggered, it grabs your GPS coordinates and opens a pre-filled emergency message with your exact location — ready to send to 911 or your emergency contacts.

Practice this now so you know how it works before you ever need it.

How SOS works:
1. Tap SOS → App grabs your GPS coordinates
2. A 5-second countdown appears (tap Cancel to abort)
3. Your phone opens a pre-filled SMS to your emergency contact with your exact location + Google Maps link
4. Hit send — help is on the way ✝
5
Set your emergency contacts
To do
▼

The SOS message will go to the phone numbers you set here. Add your spouse, a trusted neighbor, your parents — whoever should get the alert in an emergency. You can set multiple contacts.

Emergency Contact 1
Emergency Contact 2 (optional)
✓ Saved! SOS will alert these contacts.
Tip: Also save 911 or your local emergency number as a contact. In many countries Family First can pre-fill that number automatically.
6
Set up Kid Zones (safe areas)
To do
▼

Draw safe zones on the map around the places your kids go — home, school, grandma's house. The moment your child enters or leaves a zone, you get an instant notification.

🏠 Home Zone — Alert when kids arrive home after school
🏫 School Zone — Confirmed arrival and departure alerts
👴 Grandma Zone — Know when they arrive for a visit
Zone setup is available in the full app. Coming in v1.1: draw zones directly on the map from your phone. ✝
