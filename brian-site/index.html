Then listen. Let them talk. Ask one more question about what bugs them most. You're the owner, so talk like one: no script, just a conversation.
The Close
Know three things cold:

1. Your opener: your name, that you own MX Studio, and one real question about how they run their crew today. Then listen.

2. Three proof points you can show, not claim:
• Type their address and the roof measures itself.
• English and Spanish for the whole crew.
• One price, $137 a month (or $1,344 a year, save $300), for unlimited users.

3. Your close: "Let's get you set up today. If it's ever not worth it, cancel and I refund your most recent month."

Then → send the checkout link (💳 Checkout in Deal Links).
If they hesitate → "What's the one thing holding you back? Let's solve it."
Setup — While They Pay (2 min)

1️⃣ Open the app link on their phone
→ Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)
→ Tap the Share icon (box with ↑ arrow)
→ "Add to Home Screen" → Add
✅ App icon appears on their home screen

2️⃣ Open the dashboard link — same steps
✅ Dashboard icon on home screen too

3️⃣ They're set up. Show them the Jobs tab. Done.

"No App Store. No download. 2 minutes."

"Hey [Name], Brian Lalo here — sent you the JefeTech link yesterday. Did you get a chance to look?
